Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026
This policy explains what personal data PokerTen (operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGY) collects, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

1. Who is responsible

PokerTen is operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGY, which acts as the data controller for the information described below. For any privacy question or request, contact office@orizisgroup.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and phone number, provided when you sign in or register.
  • Activity in the app — the games, tables and club activity linked to your account.
  • Settings stored on your device — preferences kept in your browser's local storage so the app remembers you between visits.
  • Basic technical data — device and browser information sent automatically when you use the service.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run the service and keep your activity and history available to you.
  • To contact you about your account or support requests.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud and unauthorised access.

4. Legal basis

We process your data to perform the service you asked for, to meet our legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) for sign-in and data storage. Firebase processes your data on our behalf under Google's terms. We may also share data where the law requires it.

6. Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data except where we are legally required to keep it.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent, at any time. Email office@orizisgroup.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with access limited to authorised personnel.

9. Children

PokerTen is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: office@orizisgroup.com.
